# ApplyPeace

Sponsor-aware job applications on autopilot. ApplyPeace finds jobs at top companies (Fortune 500,
S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Global 500, AI 50 — you choose) that have a real H-1B record. An AI
shortlists them, you approve from your phone, and it applies.

> Status: in planning, nothing to run yet. The command-line tool will be called `ap`.

## Planned features

- **Guided setup:** on first run, ApplyPeace asks for your resume, standard answers and
  targets, reads the resume with AI, and has you confirm every detail.
- **Company lists you choose:** switch Fortune 500, S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Global 500, Forbes
  AI 50 and your own list on or off.
- **Official postings only:** jobs come straight from employers' own job boards, never from
  scraped aggregators.
- **H-1B evidence:** each employer's sponsorship history, from public USCIS and DOL data.
- **AI shortlist:** an LLM judge scores each job against your resume and explains why it fits.
- **Supervised auto-apply:**
  - you review and edit every answer on your phone before anything is sent;
  - applications are submitted only after you approve;
  - when a site needs a human, it hands the application back to you.
- **Full record:** every application is logged with the date, the job description, the resume
  version, the answers sent and confirmation screenshots.

ApplyPeace is not legal or immigration advice and is not affiliated with USCIS, DOL or Fortune.
